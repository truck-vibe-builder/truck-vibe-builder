# About `truck-vibe-builder`

The name captures my approach to building software: creating sturdy, functional, and practical tools ("truck-built") through AI-assisted development and interactive prompt engineering ("vibe coding").

## Who I am

I am not a professional developer and do not have formal training in computer science, though I bring a solid technical mindset and practical hands-on experience.

## What and why I build

* **Need-driven tools:** Every app I build stems from real daily needs and friction points I experience in my own workflows.
* **Platforms:** Currently focused on Linux and Windows desktop applications, with an open mind to explore other platforms in the future.
* **Lightweight and focused:** I create clean, functional utilities designed to do specific jobs well without unnecessary complexity.
